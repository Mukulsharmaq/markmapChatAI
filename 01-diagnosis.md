# Verrick Outbound Campaign Diagnosis

## The Situation

The outbound engagement has been running for 90 days with underwhelming results:
- **3.1% reply rate**
- **5 meetings booked** from 13,000 contacts
- **0.08% contact-to-meeting conversion rate**

Leadership (VP Marketing, Dev) reviewed performance and concluded: "We need more volume and should reallocate spend to the highest-reply segment (distributor)."

## The Proposal

Dev has asked for two immediate actions:
1. Buy 20,000 additional contacts
2. Double sending volume
3. Put entire budget into distributor segment (citing 13% reply rate vs 7% elsewhere)

Timeline: Board meeting in 3 weeks, needs pipeline number "that is not embarrassing."

## What the Data Actually Shows

### Segment Performance (Reply Rate ≠ Meetings)

| Segment | Reply Rate | Meetings | Reply-to-Meeting Rate |
|---------|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Distributor | 13.3% | 0 | 0% |
| Mid-market 3PL | 7.6% | 4 | 1.85% |
| Enterprise | 1.5% | 0 | 0% |
| Cold-chain | 1.5% | 1 | 4.76% |

**Key insight:** Highest reply rate does NOT correlate to pipeline. Distributor has zero meetings despite 158 replies.

### Sample of 10 Distributor Replies (Verbatim)

- "We don't actually operate our own warehouse"
- "Our 3PL handles all of this—contact them instead"
- "Product looks useful, send us a deck" (interest signal, not qualified)
- [Consultant/partnership interest, not customer interest]

**Implication:** 25-50% of distributor replies may not be actual operators.

## The Core Problem

**The campaign is optimizing for reply rate (a vanity metric) instead of outcomes (pipeline).**

The proposed scaling—adding 20k contacts and doubling volume—would amplify spend on a non-performing segment while reducing spend on the only segment generating actual meetings.

## Critical Unknowns (Must Resolve Before Scaling)

1. **Is distributor a targeting or follow-up issue?** (1-2 hours)
   - Are replies being called? Qualified? Stalled?
   - If follow-up is broken: scaling isn't the answer

2. **What's the sales cycle and deal stage on the 5 meetings?** (30 min)
   - Determines if 5 meetings in 90 days is on-track or alarming

3. **What's the overlap between new contacts and customer list?** (2-4 hours)
   - Customer list hasn't been provided yet
   - True usable size for scaling depends on this

4. **What's actually in the master file?** (1 hour)
   - Claimed 14,203 records; segment table shows 9,100
   - 5,103-record gap unexplained

5. **Can the two high-bounce domains be fixed?** (1-2 hours)
   - getverrick.com: 6.2% bounce
   - verrickhq.com: 7.8% bounce
   - Others under 2%
   - Doubling volume to these amplifies reputation damage

## Recommendation

**Do not scale to the proposal as stated.**

Instead:
- Investigate why mid-market (only working segment) is underbuying relative to distributor
- Answer unknowns #1 and #2 before any volume decision
- If forced to show pipeline by Monday: "Mid-market segment is working. Prioritizing it, running tighter follow-up on replies, holding other segments flat until we understand conversion mechanics."

The problem is diagnostic, not structural. One segment works. Others might too—but not because of reply rate.
