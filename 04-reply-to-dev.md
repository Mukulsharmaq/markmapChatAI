# Response to VP Marketing (Dev)

## Subject: Campaign Scale Recommendation

Dev,

I've reviewed the 90-day performance data and your proposal to buy 20k contacts, double volume, and reallocate to distributor.

**Bottom line: Do not scale to the proposal as stated. The data contradicts it.**

## The Issue

Your proposal optimizes for reply rate. The data shows reply rate has zero correlation to meetings.

| Segment | Reply Rate | Meetings |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Distributor (your proposed focus) | 13.3% | **0** |
| Mid-market 3PL (current performer) | 7.6% | **4** |

Scaling to distributor means spending more on a segment with zero meeting output. Scaling volume means doubling sends to high-bounce domains (6-8% vs. healthy 1-2%) and amplifying an unproven message.

The 30% labour reduction claim in the opening line is unsubstantiated by real customer examples. Distributor replies include: "we don't operate our own warehouse" and "talk to our 3PL"—these aren't operators.

## Before the Board Meeting

You have 3 weeks. Before committing budget, answer these:

1. **Follow-up quality on distributor replies** (1-2 hours)
   - Are these 158 replies being called and qualified, or just sitting?
   - If follow-up is good and conversion is still 0%, targeting is wrong.

2. **Sales cycle on the 5 meetings** (30 min)
   - What stage are they in? Expected close?
   - Tells us if 5 meetings in 90 days is on-track.

3. **Overlap with customer list** (2-4 hours)
   - You still owe us that list. Until we suppress it, we can't tell true reach.
   - Overlap could be 5-30%.

## What to Tell the Board

**Honest version:**
"Mid-market segment is working (4 meetings, 1.85% reply-to-meeting rate). We're investigating why other segments don't convert. Before scaling, we need to understand conversion mechanics, not just reply rate. Scaling prematurely would double down on the wrong metric."

**If you must show pipeline by Monday:**
"We have 4 qualified opportunities in mid-market 3PL. We're tightening follow-up on 158 distributor replies to assess qualification. We're holding volume flat on underperforming segments until we understand why reply ≠ meetings. Next board meeting: concrete conversion data for all segments."

## The Danger

If you scale to distributor as proposed:
- You'll spend more on zero-meeting conversion
- You'll reduce spend on the only working segment
- You'll amplify bounce rate issues on two domains
- You'll send unproven messaging at double volume

That's not growth. That's optimization in the wrong direction.

## What I'd Actually Do

1. Get the customer list and apply suppression (today)
2. Trace 10 distributor replies through CRM (do they get called? what stage?)
3. Check the domain reputation on the two high-bounce senders
4. Fix sequence copy based on objections (the "30% claim" is being questioned)
5. Reallocate 60% of new budget to mid-market, hold everything else flat
6. Check back in 30 days with new reply-to-meeting rates

The problem is diagnostic, not capital.
