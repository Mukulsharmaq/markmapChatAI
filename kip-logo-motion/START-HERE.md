# START HERE 👋 — your animated KIP logo sting

You gave me the **Killing Imaginary People** logo (`KIP_LogoJuly_2026.ai`) and
asked me to motion-design it for the **end of a podcast teaser**. Here it is.

## The concept

Your logo already *is* a sniper scope — concentric rings, crosshairs, corner
lock-brackets, tick marks. So the motion tells that story:

> **the scope powers on → rings & crosshair expand from the centre → a scan
> line sweeps → the target locks (flash + pulse) → the title reveals →
> everything holds, sharp and still.**

Nothing was redrawn. The exact crimson (`#e50747`), the rings, and — most
importantly — the beautiful `Imaginary` italic wordmark are the **real artwork
from your file**, so the design quality is untouched.

## The files (in `out/`)

| File | What it is | Use it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **`kip-logo-endcard.mp4`** | The full branded card **with the red background**. Just watch it — this is the hero. | You want the logo card itself as the teaser's end screen. |
| **`kip-logo-overlay.webm`** | **Transparent** version — only the white artwork animates, no background. | You want the scope + title to appear **on top of your footage**. Smaller file. |
| **`kip-logo-overlay.mov`** | Same transparent version as **ProRes 4444** (bigger, maximum quality/compatibility). | Your editor shows a black box instead of transparency with the `.webm`. |

- **1920×1080, 30 fps, 6 seconds** (≈3.7s animation + a ~2.3s hold you can trim
  or freeze-extend to whatever length your teaser needs).
- A transparent file can look blank/checkerboard in a plain media player — that's
  correct. Drop it on a track **above** your footage and it composites.

## How to use it

**As an end card (simplest):** put `kip-logo-endcard.mp4` at the end of your
teaser timeline. Done.

**As an overlay:** put your footage on the bottom track, drag
`kip-logo-overlay.webm` (or `.mov`) onto a track **above** it, line it up with
the last beat of the teaser.
